# First-Run Think-Aloud — Homepage → Onboarding (P01–P05)

**Method:** Each persona arrives cold, never having heard of Vinaadi, lands on the homepage `/`, and is followed in first-person, present tense through the hero, the page, sign-up, and the 3-step birth-details onboarding. **Verbatim inner monologue.** Every emotion and confusion surfaced; nothing skipped. **No recommendations — experience only.** The walkthrough stops the moment onboarding ends (or the moment the person gives up on it).

**Grounded in the real UI:** hero eyebrow *"Thirukanitham-Precise Tamil Astrology,"* tagline *"Your birth second, calculated precisely,"* H1 *"One calm guide for your chart, your day, and the people you plan with,"* the A/B primary CTA (*"Sign up free →"* / *"Get my jadhagam free →"* / *"Start with today's guidance →"*), the in-hero guest Rasi picker, the EN/TA toggle, three hardcoded testimonials (Ramesh K. Chennai, Lakshmi S. Madurai, Suresh M. Coimbatore), the sample "Your day" card showing score **64**, the `/login` 4-mode auth, and the setup tab: *"Three quiet steps. Then we read for you,"* the "astrology reads the weather" primer, Step 1 fields (Name · Relationship · Birth date · Birth time [optional] · **Birth Time Source** · Birth place · **Timezone** · **Latitude** · **Longitude** · Marital status · Employment type · "Calculate now — required"), Step 2 Family vault, Step 3 Add member, the Beginner/Balanced/Traditional depth picker, the app-only Premium nudge, and the disclaimer block.
