# Dashboard Think-Aloud — Inside the App (P01–P05)

**Method:** The same five personas from the P01–P05 onboarding walkthrough, continued the moment they land in the dashboard (the Nova "Today" tab). Followed first-person, present tense as they click cards, open reports, read predictions, and poke every widget — **behaving naturally, not methodically.** Confusion is explained where it happens; excitement is explained where it happens. **No redesign, no fixes — only the human's behaviour.**

**Grounded in the real dashboard:** the left rail (Today · Panchangam · Family · Tools · Explore, with depth tabs Transits/Plan/Life Areas/Journal); the Today greeting hero with the **/100 score dial** and "See why this score →"; the **Emotional weather** line + three tags (🌿 tone · 🤝 tendency · ⚠ best use of day); the panchangam band (Nakshatram · Tithi · **Nalla Neram** green · **Rahu Kalam** red · Sunrise); "**Today's one focus**" with a green "**Do it HH:MM–HH:MM**" window; the **Remedy / பரிகாரம்** card; the day-timeline ribbon; the **Decide** panel + floating **Ask Vinaadi**; "**Coming up**" (peyarchi / Sani / week-ahead / Chandrashtama flags); the Family / Dasa-chapter / Life-areas glance row; the **streak** chip; the collapsed "**Deep dive — the astrology behind today**" (chart-validation chip, activity-timing browser, planet table with pada/house/retrograde/combust/vargottama flags, **Chart Explanation**, **Nakshatra card**, **Vargas**, **Shadbala**, **Yogini / Ashtottari / Kalachakra** dashas, **Jaimini Chara Dasha**, **Annual Tajaka / Solar Return**, morning-guidance opt-in, **Prasna/Horary**, PDF download); the **Life Areas** tab (age chip, member pills, sub-tabs Overview/Predictions/Yogas & Doshams/Remedies/Full report, attention/steady/supportive tiers, chart-signature line, per-area cards with caution + remedy); the standalone **daily-score** 6-signal breakdown (moon transit 28 · gochar 24 · dasha 19 · panchangam 14 · cautions 9 · remedial 6); **Plan** (Life Event Log, What-If, **Muhurta**, **Decisions A-vs-B**, **Muhurtham Naal**); **Tools** (Porutham, Generate Chart, Annual Wrapped, Retrospective); and the pay-per-use **Reports** store.

## P01 — Murugesan "Muru" K. · 34 · auto driver, Madurai · low–medium digital · Tamil · anxious

*(In Tamil.)* It opened my page. "Maalai vanakkam, Murugesan." Aiyo it said my name. *(a small surprised smile, then immediately a suspicious glance)* — okay but is anything going to charge me. Let me keep one eye for that.

There's a big round number, coloured. This is MY number now, not the sample. It's… some amber-ish colour. Is that good? I don't know if the number is good or bad. There's a word next to it in capitals but I read English capitals slowly. *(uncertain)* If it was red I'd worry, it's not red, so maybe okay. But I'm not sure. I wish it just said "நல்ல நாள்" or "ஜாக்கிரதை" plainly.

*(He notices the panchangam strip and brightens.)* Rahu Kalam! In red, with the time. *This* I use — I never start a long airport trip in Rahu Kalam. And "Nalla Neram" in green. So today Rahu Kalam is this time — okay, I'll remember, no big trips then. This part is actually useful for me, this is the thing I know. Good. Nakshatram, tithi also there. Fine.

"Indraiya mukkiya kavanam" — today's one focus — and a green button "Seiyungal" with a time, do-it 9:40 to 11:10 or whatever. Oh. So this is the good time today? *(interested)* If this is a good-earning time I should be on the road then, near the temple or the bus stand. That I can use — a good hour to hustle. I like that it gives one clear time and not a big lecture.

"Parihaaram" — remedy. It says something small, light a lamp, say a prayer. Okay, small thing, no money, my wife does the lamp anyway. Fine. I was afraid it would say "do a 5000-rupee pooja." It didn't. Relief.

Now let me press the tabs carefully, slowly, not to press anything that costs. *(taps Family)* — empty, it says add members. I didn't add my wife and kids, I was going to ask my son. So empty. Sari, later. *(taps Tools)* — Porutham, Generate chart, and… "Reports." *(he taps Reports and immediately tenses)* — aiyo, this one has prices. Buy this report, buy that. *(he backs out fast)* No no no. This is the money part. I won't touch that. See, I knew there was a paying part somewhere. As long as it's only here and not blocking my daily number, okay. I stay away from this tab.

There's a floating button, "Ask Vinaadi," a chat. *(cautious)* Can I ask? But — does this cost my data pack, does it cost money per question? It doesn't say. *(he hesitates, then types one small thing in Tamil)* "இன்று வேலை நல்லா இருக்குமா" — will work be good today. *(waits, anxious about cost)* … It answered, calm, about the day. Okay. But I'm nervous to use it again in case each question costs. I'll leave it.

Let me open this "Deep dive — the astrology behind today," just to see. *(expands)* … Aiyaiyo. So many tables. Planets, numbers, English words everywhere. Shadbala, Vargas — I don't know these. *(overwhelmed, scrolls)* — but wait, there's the square, the kattam. That one I know! That's like the jothidar's paper, the chart. *(leans in with real interest)* That's my jaadhagam. Okay that part feels real, feels like the temple astrologer's writing. But then — *(a worry hits him)* — those latitude longitude numbers I didn't understand when I signed up… if they were wrong, is this whole kattam wrong? Is my chart correct or not? I can't tell. That uneasy feeling comes back. I really must ask my son to check those numbers.

I close the deep dive, it's too much. There's a "Life areas" thing — where you stand, area by area. *(taps)* Cards — money, health, family, with colours. The money one — *(he reads slowly, anxious)* — is it red? What does it say about money? This is the thing I worry about most. It says something gentle about the period. Not red, "steady." Okay. My chest was tight for a second — money is always my tight spot.

Enough for now. The daily number, the Rahu Kalam, the one good-time window, the small remedy — those I'll check in the morning, in Tamil, that's manageable. The tables I'll never touch, and the Reports money-tab I'll stay far from. And I have that worry sitting in me — did I put those map-numbers right, is my chart even correct. I did it all myself though, no charge yet, and it greeted me by name. *(he pockets the phone as a passenger climbs in, quietly relieved but not quite settled.)*

## P02 — Aarthi Selvaraj · 28 · engineer, Chennai · traditional mode · skeptic

Okay. I'm in. Let's audit this thing properly. I picked Traditional mode because I want the real terms, not the sanitised version — if I'm going to dismiss it I want to dismiss the actual system, not a dumbed-down one.

Today tab. Greeting, a score dial, "emotional weather," pill tags. My immediate read: this is a scoring model with a mystical skin. And — "See why this score →." Fine, show me. *(opens daily-score)* Six weighted inputs: moon transit 28, gochar 24, dasha 19, panchangam 14, cautions 9, remedial 6. *(engineer brain engages)* So it's a linear weighted sum, hand-tuned weights, normalised to 100. That's… honestly a reasonable little model. The weights are somebody's opinion with no ground truth to fit against — this is not learned, it's asserted — but at least it's *transparent* about being an asserted formula. I've seen worse rubrics ship in actual fintech. I'll give the engineering a nod even while I think the inputs are astrology nonsense. Two separate judgments.

The emotional-weather text — I read it critically. It's Barnum. "You may feel pulled between rest and action today." That applies to every human on every day. Classic cold-reading surface area. But — I notice it never says anything catastrophic or deterministic. It's carefully hedged. Someone deliberately kept it un-falsifiable *and* un-scary. From a liability standpoint that's clever; from a "is this real" standpoint it confirms my priors.

But I'm not here for the daily horoscope. I'm here for the porutham — I want to see why my parents keep saying a match "failed on Rajju." *(goes to Tools → Porutham)* Right, it needs two charts. I only have mine. Annoying — I can't fully run it without a second person's birth details, which I don't have on me. *(mild frustration)* Let me at least see the structure. It lays out the ten poruthams — Dina, Gana, Mahendra, Stree-Dirgha, Yoni, Rasi, Rasiyathipathi, Vasya, Rajju, Vedhai — and it flags **Rajju and Vedhai as hard-fail gates.** *(this actually interests her)* Okay — so *this* is the thing. My parents aren't inventing "Rajju." It's a defined gate in the traditional 10-porutham method, and if it fails the match is a hard no regardless of the other nine. So when they say "it failed on Rajju," they're not being arbitrary — they're applying this exact rule. I came here to catch them out and instead I understand the machine they're using. That's… genuinely useful to me, even if I think the underlying belief is baseless. Understanding the rule my life is being run by is worth something.

Let me open the **Deep dive** on my own chart. *(expands)* Planet table — degrees to two decimals, nakshatra, pada, house, D9 rasi, retrograde/combust/vargottama flags. As a data model this is clean and complete, I'll say that. There's a chart-validation chip — they're tracking data quality, which ties back to that birth-time-confidence field from onboarding. Consistent thinking. **Vargas, Shadbala, Yogini/Ashtottari/Kalachakra dashas.** *(skeptic's eyebrow)* This is where it stops being "a clean model" and becomes "a mountain of unfalsifiable outputs presented with total confidence." Kalachakra dasha computed to specific dates — there's no way for a user to verify any of this, and its very precision is the persuasion technique. The false precision is the tell. A number to two decimal places *feels* scientific. It isn't. I clock exactly what it's doing and I note that it's doing it well.

Life Areas — traditional mode, so it's giving me karaka reasoning, tiers, a chart signature, per-area predictions. I read the "relationships/marriage" one because obviously that's the theatre of my life right now. *(reads)* It's plausible, calm, and — of course — it references my dasha and says the period "supports" or "delays." Unfalsifiable and conveniently unprovable either way. But I notice it does NOT tell me to panic or to rush a marriage, which is more restraint than the aunties in my life show. Small credit.

I skip the Reports store — pay-per-use, I'm not paying to test a hypothesis I've already resolved. I try Ask Vinaadi with a pointed question just to see the guardrails — it's Claude, calm, hedged, won't make hard predictions. Competent wrapper.

Final assessment, staying honest: I walked in to dismiss it and I'm walking out still not believing a word of the astrology — the Barnum text, the unfalsifiable dashas, the false precision are all exactly what I expected. BUT: the score model is transparently a model, the porutham breakdown genuinely explained the Rajju rule my parents wield, and the whole thing is engineered with more discipline and less manipulation than the category deserves. I got what I actually came for — I understand the rules now — without having to convert. *(She closes the laptop, arms folded, unconverted but better-informed, which was always the real goal.)*

## P03 — Ashok Nadar · 47 · garment trader, Tiruppur · balanced mode · practical-devout

Let me try Explore, it has more. *(finds the Plan depth tab)* — ah, here: Muhurta, Muhurtham Naal, Decisions, What-If, activity timing. *This* is what I came for. *(opens Muhurta)* It gives me auspicious windows. *(opens Muhurtham Naal)* — good days by year. *(genuinely pleased)* Okay, now this is useful to me. Now the real test — does it match my purohitar? *(He mentally checks the muhurtham it's showing against what his astrologer told him for a recent date.)* … Close. **Decisions — A vs B.** *(opens, curious)* Compare two options. I have a real one: take the big buyer's order at a tight margin, or hold out for a better price and risk losing them. *(puts it in)* It compares the timing of both and gives a calm read, says it's support for decision-making, not fortune-telling. *(he respects that line)* Good — it's not pretending to be God, it's helping me think. Good, I don't need the full pandit vocabulary, I'm not a scholar, I'm a cloth man.

The **Deep dive** — *(expands, glances)* — tables, planets, Shadbala, Vargas. *(he skims, not his thing)* This is for astrologers. I'm not going to read planet degrees, that's what I pay the purohitar for. But it's good that it's there, it means the app is serious, not a toy. I close it.

**Reports** — pay-per-use. *(He looks at this differently than the others would.)* A proper porutham report, a full jadhagam report, for a price. *(considers)* Money is not my problem, time is my problem. If I need a proper printed porutham when my daughter's marriage talk starts, and I can buy it here at 11pm instead of chasing the astrologer for a week — I'll pay for that without thinking twice. I bookmark this in my mind. This is fine. This is a fair deal for a busy man.

I don't add family now — my wife, my son, later, when I have their exact birth times, my mother knows. Right now I've seen what I need.

My verdict: the front page kept talking about "calm daily life and family" and I almost thought it wasn't for me — but underneath, in Plan and Muhurta and Decisions, there's exactly the business timing I wanted, and it roughly matches my purohitar, and I can reach it any hour. That's the whole value for me. The daily mood-poetry I'll ignore, the astrologer tables I'll ignore, but the muhurtham and the decision tool — those I'll use, and I'll probably pay for a report when the time comes. *(He nods to himself, already thinking about next month's launch date, and reaches for his buyer's file.)*
