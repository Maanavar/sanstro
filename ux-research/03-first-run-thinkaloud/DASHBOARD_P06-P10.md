# Dashboard Think-Aloud — Inside the App (P06–P10)

**Method:** The same five personas from the P06–P10 onboarding walkthrough, continued the moment they land in the dashboard (the Nova "Today" tab). Followed first-person, present tense as they click cards, open reports, read predictions, and poke every widget — **behaving naturally, not methodically.** Confusion is explained where it happens; excitement is explained where it happens. **No redesign, no fixes — only the human's behaviour.**

**Grounded in the real dashboard:** the left rail (Today · Panchangam · Family · Tools · Explore, with depth tabs Transits/Plan/Life Areas/Journal); the Today greeting hero with the **/100 score dial** and "See why this score →"; the **Emotional weather** line + three tags (🌿 tone · 🤝 tendency · ⚠ best use of day); the panchangam band (Nakshatram · Tithi · **Nalla Neram** green · **Rahu Kalam** red · Sunrise); "**Today's one focus**" with a green "**Do it HH:MM–HH:MM**" window; the **Remedy / பரிகாரம்** card; the day-timeline ribbon; the **Decide** panel + floating **Ask Vinaadi**; "**Coming up**" (peyarchi / Sani / week-ahead / Chandrashtama flags); the Family / Dasa-chapter / Life-areas glance row; the **streak** chip; the collapsed "**Deep dive — the astrology behind today**" (chart-validation chip, activity-timing browser, planet table with pada/house/retrograde/combust/vargottama flags, **Chart Explanation**, **Nakshatra card** with strength/caution chips, **Vargas**, **Shadbala**, **Yogini / Ashtottari / Kalachakra** dashas, **Jaimini Chara Dasha**, **Annual Tajaka / Solar Return** with SR Lagna + Muntha, morning-guidance opt-in, **Prasna/Horary** trigger, PDF download); the **Life Areas** tab ("Where you stand, area by area," age chip, member pills, sub-tabs Overview/Predictions/Yogas & Doshams/Remedies/Full report, the attention/steady/supportive tiers, chart-signature line, per-area cards with caution + remedy); the standalone **daily-score** 6-signal breakdown (moon transit 28 · gochar 24 · dasha 19 · panchangam 14 · cautions 9 · remedial 6); **Plan** (Life Event Log, What-If, Muhurta, Decisions A-vs-B, Muhurtham Naal); **Tools** (Porutham, Generate Chart, Annual Wrapped, Retrospective); and the pay-per-use **Reports** store.
