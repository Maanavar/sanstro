# Top 100 UX Issues — Evidence Synthesis

**Role:** UX Research Lead
**Date:** 2026-07-07
**Sources read (all persona reviews):**
- `02-personas/` — 60 persona profiles (P01–P60), incl. goals, frustrations, reasons-for-use
- `03-first-run-thinkaloud/` — 10 observed first-run walkthroughs (P01–P10), onboarding **and** dashboard, verbatim
- `04-feature-testing/` — feature-by-feature, one-per-persona, and surface-by-surface tests (P01–P10 observed; P11–P60 needs mapped)

**Rules applied:** Group similar complaints → count frequency → rank by severity → evidence only, no invented issues, no personal design opinions. Every row cites at least one persona and quote/behaviour from the corpus.

---

## Legend & method

**Evidence type**
- **O = Observed** — a P01–P10 persona hit this in a recorded think-aloud/feature test (verbatim quote exists).
- **I = Inferred-from-profile** — a P11–P60 persona's documented goal/frustration/reason-for-use directly implicates this issue (they were not walked through the UI, but their stated need makes the issue apply).
- **R = Risk / unverified** — the reviews flag a need whose implementation was *not* confirmed in-session; treated as a risk to verify, not a proven defect. Marked in the row.

**Frequency** = number of the 60 personas for whom the issue is evidenced or clearly implicated, shown as `total (O / I)`. This is a reach estimate, not a defect count.

**Severity**
- **Critical** — blocks the persona's core reason for being in the app, or excludes an entire segment from using it at all.
- **High** — materially damages trust or drives likely abandonment for a segment; workaround is costly or absent.
- **Medium** — friction, confusion, or unmet secondary need; a workaround exists.
- **Low** — minor, cosmetic, or narrow.

**Ranking** = severity tier first, then frequency, then breadth of business impact. Ranks are grouped into tiers; within a tier the order reflects reach × impact.

**Grouping tags** (the complaint clusters):
`LANG` language/localisation · `READ` literacy/modality · `ONB` onboarding/form · `TRUST` trust/credibility · `PRIV` privacy/data · `SCORE` daily score · `CONTENT` content/tone · `RELIG` religion fit · `CHAT` Ask Vinaadi · `FAMILY` family vault · `DEEP` deep-dive/rigor · `AREAS` life areas · `MONEY` monetisation · `TIME` timing/muhurtham · `SEG` unmet segment need · `A11Y` accessibility · `NAV` navigation/discoverability · `SAFE` liability/safeguard.

## Part C — What the ranking says (evidence-level, not opinion)

1. **Comprehension and modality, not accuracy, dominate the critical tier.** Six of the top nine issues are about *whether the user can read/operate the thing at all* (text-only UI, English default, opaque score, exposed lat/long, email wall, paragraph-only chat). The engine's correctness — which the one expert largely validated — is a Tier-2/3 concern by comparison. *Evidence: P09, P01, P07, P08 across ONB/DASH; FEAT §"Legibility, not accuracy, decides adoption."*

2. **The widest single fault line is religion + locale.** The Hindu-ritual remedy/framing (#10, #11) reaches ~14 non-Hindu personas, and the diaspora locale/timezone risk (#2, #12, #37) reaches ~20 — together the largest reach in the register. Both are structural, not cosmetic. *Evidence: P05, P06 observed; P29, P51, P57, P59 profiles.*

3. **Trust is gated upstream of revenue.** The chart-foundation doubt (#3), data-custody fear (#7), and hidden-charges reflex (#19) all sit *before* any paid surface; the two personas who would pay (P03, P08) only do so after manually cross-checking accuracy the app never proves for them (#45). *Evidence: P01, P04 observed; FEAT surface-9 conclusion.*

4. **The expert-trust cracks are few but high-leverage.** Only ~2–3 personas (P08, and inferred P11) can see the Shadbala mislabel (#20) and uncaveated Kalachakra (#21) — but they are the credibility-setting authority tier whose verdict travels. Low frequency, high blast radius. *Evidence: P08 observed; corroborated by project memory on experimental engines.*

5. **A long tail of unmet segment jobs is real and evidenced, not padding.** Agricultural muhurtham (#35), facilitator tooling (#36), remote participation (#46), ritual/memorial days (#66), education timing (#44), fertility sensitivity (#77), learning on-ramp (#48) — each is the *stated core reason-to-use* of a specific persona, drawn from `02-personas`. These are opportunities as much as defects.

## Method notes & honesty flags

- **Observed vs. inferred is kept explicit.** Only P01–P10 were walked through the UI; every P11–P60 citation is an `I` (profile-implied) or `R` (unverified risk). Roughly 30 of the 100 rest primarily on inferred/profile evidence — they are ranked lower or flagged accordingly, never presented as observed defects.
- **Frequency is a *reach* estimate, not a bug count.** It counts personas the issue plausibly affects, from documented profiles — useful for prioritisation, not a statistical incidence rate (n=60 synthetic personas, 10 observed).
- **`R`-tagged rows are risks to verify, not confirmed defects** (timezone handling, notification behaviour, regional calendars, Wrapped share, combined-family view). The reviews flag the *need*; implementation was not confirmed in-session. These should be checked against the build before being actioned as bugs.
- **Positives were deliberately excluded** per the brief (issues only), but the corpus is not uniformly negative — the panchangam strip, the 6-signal score breakdown, the "weather not verdict" framing, and the birth-time-confidence field all drew unprompted praise across personas and are the assets these fixes should protect.
- **No personal design opinions or fixes are included** — this is a read on the evidence. Remediation/prioritisation is a separate exercise.
