# Personas — Index & Method

**Author:** Senior Product Researcher
**Date:** 2026-07-07
**Purpose:** A library of 60 realistic Tamil personas to ground later UX research, prioritisation, and content decisions. **This is people-only** — no product review, no recommendations. Each persona is a plausible human, not a segment or a stereotype.

## What each persona contains
Age · Occupation · Country · Family · Income · Digital literacy · Astrology knowledge · Goals · Frustrations · Technology usage · Personality · Reason for using astrology. Plus a name and a short "in their words" line to keep them human.

## Anti-stereotype rules used while writing these
- **Belief is not a monolith.** The set spans devout practitioners, casual/cultural users, anxious coping-users, curious agnostics, hard rationalists (Tamil Nadu's Periyarist tradition), and outright skeptics who still open the app. Not everyone "believes."
- **Religion is mixed.** Tamil Hindus, Tamil Christians (Catholic + Protestant), and Tamil Muslims all appear; astrology use varies within each, not by cliché.
- **Digital literacy is decoupled from age and class.** There are tech-fluent 70-year-olds and low-literacy young adults, wealthy people who can barely type and low-income people who live on their phones.
- **Occupation is not destiny.** Traders aren't all shrewd, homemakers aren't all uneducated, engineers aren't all skeptics.
- **Gender balance ≈ 50/50**, with life stages the product actually age-gates (a minor via parent, students, young adults, mid-life, seniors, widowed).
- **Geography reflects the real Tamil footprint:** Tamil Nadu core, rest of India, Sri Lanka, Malaysia, Singapore, the Gulf labour diaspora, and the Western professional diaspora (UK/US/Canada/Australia), plus older diaspora (Mauritius, France/Puducherry heritage).

## How to use these
- Pressure-test flows against the **range**, not the average: a flow that works for P10 (skeptic founder) and P09 (low-literacy fisherman) and P37 (74-yr-old on a tablet) is robust.
- Watch the **belief spectrum** especially — the product's calm, non-fatalistic tone is aimed at anxious-coping and skeptic-adjacent users; the devout have different expectations of authority and orthodoxy.
- Every persona is **synthetic**. No real birth data, names, or coordinates are used.
