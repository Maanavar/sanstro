# Surface-by-Surface Testing — Nine Named Surfaces (across P01–P10)

**Method:** Same six questions as the companion files, run one surface at a time across the ten observed personas:

1. **What they expected** · 2. **What happened** · 3. **Understood it?** · 4. **Trust it?** · 5. **Return tomorrow?** · 6. **Pay for it?**

**Grounding note:** Horoscope, Compatibility, Muhurtham, Reports, Panchangam, and Daily/Weekly scores were all directly observed in the P01–P10 first-run think-alouds. **Settings, Notifications, and Profile were *not* walked in those sessions** — the app has them (reading-mode Beginner/Balanced/Traditional, language `ta-en`, notification prefs, the `/notifications` inbox, and the edit-profile / birth-details modal with birth-time-confidence + rectification + delete), but persona reactions to them are marked **[inferred]** from established traits rather than **[observed]**. No redesign; behaviour and judgement only.

## Surface 1 — Horoscope (full natal reading: planets, positions, dasha, yogham, pariharam & more)

*The whole jadhagam: Deep Dive planet table (degrees, nakshatra, pada, house, retro/combust/vargottama), Chart Explanation, Vargas, Shadbala, Vimshottari + Yogini/Ashtottari/Kalachakra/Chara dashas, Yogas & Doshams, per-area Remedies, PDF.*

- **What they expected:** P08 (hobbyist) expected shown workings and correct ayanamsa, ready to catch an error instantly. P04/P02 wanted to *see* the reasoning behind verdicts. Folk users (P01, P07) expected nothing this deep and hoped only to recognise their chart.
- **What happened:** For P08 the core **passed his audit** (Lahiri correct, padas/combustion/vargottama shown, Moon nakshatra/pada + Lagna verified) but the edges failed — **Shadbala simplified while wearing the full name; Kalachakra shipped prominently without a caveat.** For everyone else it was a wall of tables: P01 "aiyaiyo, so many"; P05 "a different app hiding under the calm one"; P06 "the PhD section"; P07 closed it "a little embarrassed." The one universal touch-point: P01 and P09 recognised the **kattam** (chart grid) as "the astrologer's paper."
- **Understood it?** **Fully only P08** (and unobserved P11 priest / P19-type). Partly P02/P04 (the plain "Chart Explanation" and Yogas/Doshams, not the tables). **No** for P01, P05, P06, P07, P09.
- **Trust it?** P08: core **yes** (matches his hand-work today), edges **no** (mislabelled Shadbala, unverified Kalachakra) — "a serious instrument whose ambition outruns its rigor at the edges." Folk users trust only the *shape* they recognise (the kattam), not the content they can't read. P01 carries a live doubt: "if my lat/long were wrong, is the whole chart wrong?"
- **Return tomorrow?** **Yes — for P08 specifically and powerfully** (it gave him Kalachakra to interrogate against his texts). No daily return from anyone else — a natal chart is a read-once artefact, not a daily surface.
- **Pay for it?** **P08 maybe** (a full jadhagam PDF to study grandchildren's charts), **P03 yes** (a proper printed report bought at 11pm rather than chasing the astrologer). Not the daily majority. This is the moat-and-liability surface: nobody else computes this depth, and nobody's verified the edges.

## Surface 2 — Compatibility (Porutham, 10-factor with Rajju/Vedhai gates)

- **What they expected:** P02 (skeptic) came to decode *why* matches "fail on Rajju." P04 (mother) wanted a clear, non-exploitative second opinion for her daughter's match.
- **What happened:** All ten poruthams shown, Rajju/Vedhai flagged as hard-fail gates. P02: "I came to catch my parents out and instead I understand the machine." P04: "the proper traditional method, not a broker's shortcut — I can see all ten myself." **Both blocked from actually running it** — no second person's chart on hand.
- **Understood it?** **Yes** — P02 read it as a rules engine; P04, the maths teacher, followed all ten factors and the gate logic.
- **Trust it?** Trusted as a **faithful implementation of the method** (not as truth). P02 rejects the belief, trusts the rules are applied correctly; P04 trusts the completeness + calm, non-upselling tone.
- **Return tomorrow?** P04 **yes** (deferred until she adds her daughter). P02 **no** — question resolved, dissect-and-leave.
- **Pay for it?** P04 **maybe** ("only if the free porutham isn't enough"). P02 no. The real porutham-report buyers are the *facilitators* — P03 (marriage talk), and unobserved P16 (wedding planner) whose critical path it sits on.

## Surface 3 — Muhurtham (auspicious-timing lookup: Muhurta + Muhurtham Naal)

- **What they expected:** P03 wanted an always-available muhurtham so he isn't waiting days for his astrologer before a business move.
- **What happened:** Auspicious windows + good-days-by-year, and — decisively — **it roughly matched his purohitar** for a recent date ("same day is green"). P03 flipped from "front page isn't for me" to "more than interested."
- **Understood it?** **Yes** — his domain, zero learning curve.
- **Trust it?** **Yes, roughly** — the match with his own astrologer is exactly what converted him. "Same day green, in my pocket at midnight" clears his bar.
- **Return tomorrow?** **Yes, on demand** — whenever a signing/launch/family date arises.
- **Pay for it?** **Yes — the single highest willingness-to-pay in the ten.** "Money is not my problem, time is." This surface + Reports is the app's clearest revenue engine, and the true home of unobserved P15/P16/P18/P22 (farmer, planner, jeweller, dancer).

## Surface 4 — Reports (pay-per-use store)

- **What they expected:** Most *encountered* it rather than sought it. P01 stumbled on it; P03 sized it up as a busy man's convenience.
- **What happened:** Jadhagam reports, porutham reports, Ask-Vinaadi top-ups, all priced. P01 **fled**: "aiyo, the money part — I stay far from this tab." P03: "buy a proper porutham at 11pm instead of chasing the astrologer for a week — I'll pay without thinking twice." P05/P06 "prices, skip." P10 (founder) admired the "freemium + subscription + pay-per-use" stack.
- **Understood it?** **Yes, instantly** — everyone understood "these cost money." Reaction, not comprehension, is what splits.
- **Trust it?** P01's "hidden charges" fear makes him distrust the whole *tab* reflexively — but he's relieved it's quarantined and "not blocking my daily number." P03 trusts it as a fair deal. Trust here = money-anxiety vs. time-anxiety.
- **Return tomorrow?** Not a daily surface by design — returned to *on need* (P03 at marriage talk, P08 for a report to study).
- **Pay for it?** **Lands on 2 of 10:** P03 (yes, readily), P08 (maybe, serious reports). Everyone else a firm no — folk users on money-anxiety (P01, P07, P09), skeptics won't-pay-to-test (P02), reflective users on guilt/low-commitment (P05, P06). Willingness-to-pay = time-rich/cash-fine + serious hobbyist, never the daily majority.

## Surface 5 — Panchangam (Nakshatram · Tithi · Nalla Neram · Rahu Kalam · Sunrise + full tab)

- **What they expected:** The folk-devout (P01, P07, P09) came *for this* — the caution/good hours they already live by. P08 expected to audit it against his own almanac.
- **What happened:** Rahu Kalam (red, exact times) and Nalla Neram (green) at the top; a full-panchangam tab with the day + calendar. P01 mapped it to real life in seconds; P07 "this is what I came for"; P08 "tithi, nakshatra, Rahu Kalam **to the minute**, matches my panjangam"; P09 (fisherman) leaned in for the first time.
- **Understood it?** **Yes — the most universally legible surface in the app.** Even P09, who can't read the UI, understands the times when pointed to them. (P07 briefly confused what Rahu Kalam *governs*, not the times.)
- **Trust it?** **Highest trust of any surface** — matches the temple almanac (P01, P07) and the expert's own panjangam to the minute (P08). Reads as "real," same category as the priest's paper.
- **Return tomorrow?** **Yes — the strongest daily habit in the app.** P01 (morning, before a big-fare day), P07 ("every morning like the lamp"). The retention spine for the folk-devout majority.
- **Pay for it?** **No — from anyone.** Panchangam is free public/temple knowledge in every folk mental model. P01 fled the moment a price appeared. A load-bearing daily surface that is structurally un-monetisable — and shouldn't try to be.

## Surface 6 — Daily / Weekly scores (the /100 dial + 6-signal breakdown + week-ahead)

- **What they expected:** Folk users (P01, P07) wanted a plain "good or bad day" — நல்ல நாள் / ஜாக்கிரதை. Analysts (P02, P08, P10) expected a mystical number to dismiss, best case shown workings.
- **What happened:** An amber /100 dial + English caps label, "See why →" opening six weighted signals (moon transit 28 · gochar 24 · dasha 19 · panchangam 14 · cautions 9 · remedial 6); a week-ahead outlook in "Coming up." The dial confused P01/P07/P09 ("is amber good? is 60 pass marks?"). The *breakdown* converted the analysts: P10 "the single smartest trust move in the app"; P02 "a reasonable little weighted model, honestly asserted"; P08 approved the Chandra-transit-dominant weighting.
- **Understood it?** The **dial: no** for P01/P07/P09 (opaque colour + English label), **yes** for P02/P08/P10. The **breakdown: yes** for the analysts, glazed for P06, unreached by folk users. Weekly framing understood by the planners (P03, P10) more than by daily-glance users.
- **Trust it?** The bare number earns *no* trust from anyone (even P08 calls it "astrology-for-children"). Trust appears *only after* the breakdown opens — transparency, not the score, is the trust object. "It reframes 'do you believe in astrology' into 'judge this rubric yourself.'" (P10)
- **Return tomorrow?** The score isn't why anyone returns — folk users return for the panchangam beside it, analysts for the breakdown beneath it. Weekly view is a light planning re-open for P03/P10, not a daily hook.
- **Pay for it?** No — the free hook and retention/trust layer, correctly un-monetised.

## Surface 7 — Settings (reading mode · language · notification prefs · morning-guidance opt-in) *[inferred]*

- **What they expected:** *[observed at onboarding]* Mode choice was made and mattered: P02 & P08 deliberately picked **Traditional** ("I want the real terms, not the sanitised version"); P05 & P06 picked **Beginner** ("I don't want to feel dumb"); P03 & P10 sat in **Balanced**. So the one setting that demonstrably shaped every session is the reading-mode selector. *[inferred]* Beyond mode, low-digital users don't expect a settings area at all.
- **What happened:** *[observed]* Mode did real work — Traditional gave P08 karaka reasoning and the full jargon; Beginner kept P05/P07's language plain. *[inferred]* Language defaults to `ta-en`, which suits the Tamil-first users (P01, P04, P07, P09) without action. Morning-guidance opt-in and notification prefs are buried and would go untouched by most.
- **Understood it?** *[observed]* The **mode selector: yes** — everyone understood "how much detail / jargon." *[inferred]* The rest of settings: **not found** by P01/P07/P09 (won't hunt for a gear icon); understood-if-found by P02/P08/P10.
- **Trust it?** *[inferred]* Neutral-utility — settings aren't a trust object. The one trust-relevant effect is *negative-avoidance*: Beginner mode is what keeps P05/P07 from hitting the jargon wall and losing trust. Mode is a trust *safeguard*, not a trust *builder*.
- **Return tomorrow?** *[inferred]* No — settings is a set-once surface. Once mode/language are right, no one returns to it. (Exception: P08 might toggle modes to compare depth; P01 might revisit *only* to find "make it more Tamil / bigger text" if such an option existed.)
- **Pay for it?** No — infrastructure, not a feature. But note: the **mode setting is quietly load-bearing** — a wrong default (Traditional for P05, Beginner for P08) would have soured both sessions. Its value is protecting every other feature's reception.

## Surface 8 — Notifications (the `/notifications` inbox + unread count + push) *[inferred]*

- **What they expected:** *[inferred from traits]* P07 (family almanac) and P19-type (tracking mother/kids' Sani/Chandrashtama days) would *want* proactive alerts — "tell me the festival day, my husband's caution travel-day." P05 (shift worker) would want gentle timing nudges. P01/P09, by contrast, expect notifications to mean **spam or cost** — the exact thing that makes P01 anxious.
- **What happened:** *[inferred]* A quiet inbox (mark-all-read, typed items) with push registration. For the wanting-segment it's the right idea but empty/underused at first run; for the anxious segment, any push at all risks reading as intrusion. None of the observed personas actually reached this page in their session.
- **Understood it?** *[inferred]* **Yes** — an inbox with unread badges is a universal pattern; even low-digital users know a bell icon from WhatsApp. Comprehension isn't the issue; *willingness to allow it* is.
- **Trust it?** *[inferred]* Split on the same money/spam fault line as everywhere else. P07 would trust a gentle festival/caution-day reminder (it mirrors what she phones the temple for). P01 would distrust any push that might "cost my data pack" or nag him — his whole posture is fear of being charged or pestered.
- **Return tomorrow?** *[inferred]* Notifications don't *pull* a return — they *manufacture* one. For the almanac-devout (P07, P19-type, P29-type) a well-timed festival/Rahu-Kalam/caution-day push is arguably the strongest possible re-engagement lever the app has. For the anxious-poor it's a risk of opt-out or uninstall. High-leverage, high-risk, entirely dependent on tone and frequency.
- **Pay for it?** No — a retention channel, not a paid feature. Its ROI is bringing the panchangam/Muhurtham users *back*, i.e. it monetises indirectly by feeding Surfaces 3–5.

## Surface 9 — Profile (edit birth details: date/time/place/lat-long, birth-time source + confidence, marital status, rectification, delete) *[inferred + observed]*

- **What they expected:** *[observed]* Nobody came *for* this — it's the chore behind everything else. But it surfaced two of the sharpest anxieties in the whole study: P01's lat/long doubt ("those latitude-longitude numbers I didn't understand at signup — if they're wrong, is my whole chart wrong?") and P04's privacy fear ("my daughter's private birth details in a company's computer — in our custom you don't hand a horoscope to strangers").
- **What happened:** *[observed]* At signup the birth-details form (with a birth-time-confidence field P02 and P08 both noticed and respected as "tracking data quality") did its job — but left P01 unsure he'd entered coordinates correctly, and left P04 unwilling to enter her daughter *at all* until she resolves the privacy question. *[inferred]* Rectification and delete-profile controls exist but weren't discovered in-session.
- **Understood it?** *[observed]* Partial. The obvious fields (name, date, place) yes; **lat/long is the comprehension crack** — P01 genuinely doesn't know if his are right, and there's no reassurance loop. *[inferred]* Birth-time source/confidence is understood-and-appreciated by the analysts (P02, P08), opaque to folk users.
- **Trust it?** *[observed]* **This surface is where trust is most fragile.** Two things converge: *data correctness* (P01 can't verify his own chart's foundation) and *data custody* (P04 won't hand over a private jathagam). Both are trust blockers that sit *upstream* of every other feature — a shaky profile poisons the horoscope, the porutham, everything.
- **Return tomorrow?** *[observed]* Only under duress — P01 "must ask my son to check those numbers," P04 "will sleep on the privacy of it before entering Deepa." Return here is anxious maintenance, not engagement.
- **Pay for it?** No — but it's the **highest-stakes free surface**: if the profile is wrong or feels unsafe, nothing downstream (including the paid reports) can be trusted. Its job is to *earn* the trust that Surfaces 1–4 spend.

## What surface-by-surface testing reveals

1. **The nine surfaces sort into three jobs, and only one of them makes money.** *Trust-and-retain* (Panchangam, Scores, Emotional Weather, Settings, Notifications, Profile) — load-bearing but structurally free. *Convert-and-decode* (Horoscope, Compatibility) — came-for value, trusted as *method*, mostly one-time. *Monetise* (Muhurtham, Reports) — and willingness-to-pay lands on just **P03 and P08**. The revenue surface is narrow and the retention surface is wide; that's the correct shape, but it means pricing must never touch the daily almanac.
2. **The three unglamorous surfaces (Settings, Notifications, Profile) are trust *infrastructure*, not features.** Reading-mode is quietly load-bearing — a wrong default would have soured P05 or P08's entire session. Profile is the most fragile surface in the app: P01's unverifiable lat/long and P04's data-custody fear sit *upstream* of everything and can poison even the paid reports. Notifications is the single highest-leverage re-engagement lever *and* the one most likely to trigger uninstall among the anxious-poor. None of them monetise; all of them gate whether the rest works.
3. **Comprehension, not belief, is still the dividing line.** Panchangam wins everywhere because everyone can read it; the Horoscope loses 5 of 10 because it's a wall; the score dial loses the folk majority to an opaque number while the *breakdown* wins the analysts. Every surface's fate is set by whether its core user can read it, not whether they believe it.
4. **Profile is where the whole trust chain is anchored — and it's the weakest link.** P01 can't verify his coordinates; P04 won't surrender her daughter's details. Until those two anxieties are answered, the horoscope, compatibility, and reports they feed are all built on ground the user doesn't trust. This surface deserves the most attention despite being the least "product."
