The review is input, not a sign-off: it is not you and not a native-speaker pass, and it
was wrong in places (see the end of this sheet).

**Fixed, whatever you decide below:**

1. **ராகு காலம் for a Rahu dasa.** Readings wrote "{planet} காலம்", so a Rahu dasa came out as
   "ராகு காலம் தொடங்கும்", which a Tamil reader takes for the daily inauspicious hour. The astrologer
   ruled on this on 2026-10-21, and it had slipped through. Every period name is now
   சுக்கிர தசை / ராகு புக்தி / சந்திர மகாதசை, in the readings, in the dasa-change notification
   (which used to say "ராகு காலம் ஆரம்பம்") and in two risk notes. A test now blocks the old form.
2. **The twenty-minute line** said a twenty-minute error always moves the lagna. It only does
   near a sign change, and the line now says so.
3. **The 2025 turn** (A5) announced a change without saying what it was. It now names the new bhukti.
4. **A child's learning "gets easier after Dec 2027"** was an engine error. The slowness comes
   from the mahadasha (Mars), but the date given was the bhukti's end, and the beat beside it
   correctly called that bhukti the easy part. The warning is now dated to the end of the
   mahadasha, and it no longer promises that the next one is easier, because nothing has scored it.
5. **Avittam's symbol** was "elephant tusk / drum" in both languages. The tusk belongs to Pooradam
   and Uthiradam. It is now மத்தளம் / Drum, and Revathi's drum is மத்தளம் too (மேளம் is an ensemble).
6. **Rewording** of the lines the review rightly called translated: A1, A2, A4, A5, A7, A10,
   A11, A14, A17–A20, A22, A25, A26 in v1 numbering.
7. **Every reading is printed in full**, with no "…", so you can judge how each sentence joins the next.

**Not touched, because they carry your 2026-08-12 approval:** the lines in B1. Each now comes
