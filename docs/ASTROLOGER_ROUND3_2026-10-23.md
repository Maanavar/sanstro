## R1. The neechabhanga test, as written, fires in every chart

You ruled (B1b) that debility cancels only under neechabhanga: "the lord of
Karka (Chandra) or of Makara (Sani) in a kendra from Lagna **or Moon**".

Taken literally, Chandra is always in the 1st house from the Moon — it *is* the
Moon. So "Chandra in a kendra from the Moon" is true of every chart ever cast,
the bhanga always holds, and debility would always cancel. That is the opposite
of what the same answer rules.

We therefore judge **Chandra from the Lagna only**, and Sani from either the
Lagna or the Moon.

- **R1.** Is that right? Or did you mean something else by "from Moon" for
  Chandra — for example Chandra in a kendra from the *debilitated Mars*, or
  Chandra strong by some other test?

## R2. Vaikunta Ekadashi and the Dwadasi fast

You ruled (A6a) that when an Ekadashi holds no sunrise the fast is on the
Dwadasi, with the almanac's day shown as a second line.

Before we move the printed day, one case gives us pause. **Vaikunta Ekadashi
2025 held no sunrise.** The day it fell in was 30 December 2025, and that is
the morning Srirangam opened the Paramapada Vasal (about 05:45). Both printed
almanacs give 30 December too. The Dwadasi was 31 December.

If the app prints the Dwadasi as "Vaikunta Ekadashi", it will name a different
day from the temple and from the almanac for the biggest Ekadashi of the year.

- **R2a.** Does the Dwadasi rule apply to Vaikunta Ekadashi as well, or is the
  Sorgavasal day (the day the tithi falls in) the printed day for that one?
- **R2b.** If the Dwadasi rule applies throughout, should the *printed calendar
  day* still be the almanac's day, with the Dwadasi shown as "fasting day"? That
  is the distinction you drew in your first answer (Q9a), and it would let us
  keep both without contradicting the temple.

We have not changed the Ekadashi day yet; it is still the day the tithi falls in.
