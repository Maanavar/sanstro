# Questions for the astrologer: correcting an unknown birth time (2026-11-12)

**From:** the Vinaadi team. **About:** the tool that tries to work out a birth time from
events the reader remembers (நேர திருத்தம் / birth-time rectification).
A short answer under each question is enough. Where you use a rule from a book, please give
the book, publisher, edition and page, so we can cite it.

All examples come from made-up test charts, not real people.

---

## Why we are asking

**We switched this tool off.** Until 2026-11-10 the app would suggest a birth time, and the
reader could save it — after which every reading in the app used it. The way it chose that
time was wrong: it gave a point whenever the candidate's **lagna rasi number** happened to
match the **house number** of the event. So a திருச்சி (Thulam) or ரிஷபம் lagna "supported"
every marriage, in every chart, and **the dates the reader typed were never looked at at
all**. Shuffling the dates changed nothing.

So we removed the suggestion and the save. We still keep the events the reader enters.
Nothing is shown to them now.

We will not switch it back on with a method we invented. Below is what we would propose;
**please correct it, or replace it with how you actually do this.** If your answer is that
this cannot be done honestly from a handful of remembered events, that is a complete and
acceptable answer — we would then keep the tool off and say so plainly to readers.

---

## 1. First, and most important: is this how it is done at all?

We have assumed a method where the app tries each possible birth time in turn and asks, for
each remembered event, whether that chart "explains" the event — through the house for that
event, the running தசை/புக்தி, the relevant வர்கம், and the slow transits.

- **Is that how you would correct an unknown birth time?**
- If not, what do you do instead? (For example: from the நட்சத்திரம் of the moment of
  birth as reported by the family, from the மாந்தி, from a prasna, from the reader's
  physical features and lagna, or something else entirely.)
- Is there a Tamil method for this that we should be following rather than the
  north-Indian/dasha-matching approach we have assumed?

**If your method is different, stop here and describe yours — the rest of this sheet is
about ours, and we would rather build yours.**

## 3. The weights: what counts, and how much?

This is the part we most need from you. For one candidate birth time and one remembered
event, we proposed giving points like this. **The owner has not approved this table and we
have not built it.**

| Test on the candidate chart | We proposed | Your answer: how many points, or "not used" |
|---|---|---|
| The event's house, its lord and the lagna lord are linked (by occupation, aspect or lordship) | 1 | |
| The running **மகாதசை / புக்தி** lord is linked to that house or its lord | 2 | |
| The **பிரத்யந்தர** lord is linked as well | 1 | |
| The **வர்கம்** repeats it (நவாம்சம் for marriage, சப்தாம்சம் for a child, தசாம்சம் for career) | 1 | |
| A slow transit at the event date (குரு, சனி, or both on the house) | 1 | |
| Something you would add | | |

- Is the **தசை/புக்தி** really the strongest leg, as we have it at 2 points? Or is the
  house-and-lord link the stronger one?
- Should any single test be able to **rule a candidate out** altogether, rather than just
  score less?
- Would you ever let **transits alone** decide? We have assumed not — transits and dasa can
  *support* a time but cannot *prove* one. Do you agree?

## 4. How sure is the reader's date?

When someone cannot remember their birth time, they often cannot remember an event date
exactly either. We plan to ask the reader, for each event, how sure they are: **the exact
day / only the month / only the year / not sure**.

Our reasoning: a தசை or புக்தி can be tested only if we know the date closely, because the
period boundaries move. A year-only date cannot carry that test.

| Reader's certainty | Tests we would allow | Your answer |
|---|---|---|
| Exact day | all of them | |
| Month only | house-and-lord, மகாதசை/புக்தி, slow transit — **not** பிரத்யந்தர, **not** வர்கம் | |
| Year only | house-and-lord only | |
| Not sure | shown back to the reader, but scored for nothing | |

Is that the right place to draw each line?

---

## 5. When should the app refuse to answer?

We think refusing is the normal outcome, not a failure. We proposed refusing to suggest any
time when **any** of these is true:

- fewer than **three** events known to the month or better;
- the best candidate time is too close to the second-best to tell apart;
- the leading candidates fall in **more than one lagna** (then we cannot even name a lagna,
  which is the one thing most readings need).

Questions:

- Is **three events** the right minimum? Would you want more, or different *kinds* of event
  (say, at least two different areas of life rather than three jobs)?
- If the leading candidates all sit inside one lagna but in different நவாம்சம், is that a
  usable answer ("your lagna is X, the exact minute is open"), or still a refusal?

---

## 6. How precise may we claim to be?

We would search in 30-minute steps first, then look more closely around the best few.

Our worry: the **நவாம்சம் lagna changes roughly every 13 minutes**. So an answer given to
the nearest 5 minutes may be claiming more precision than remembered life events can
support, even when the arithmetic produces it.

- What is the **finest** window you would be willing to state to a reader from this kind of
  evidence — the lagna only, the நவாம்சம், or an actual time to the minute?
- Would you show the reader a **range** ("between 10:40 and 11:10 in the morning") rather
  than a single time? That is our preference.

---

## 7. What should the reader be told, and be allowed to do?

- We will never call the result "your correct birth time". We planned the wording
  "the time that best fits the events you told us". Is that honest enough in your view?
- Should the reader be able to **save** that time, so the rest of the app uses it? Today
  they cannot. If yes, what should we warn them about first?
- Should a reader whose birth time is unknown see this tool **at all** before you have
  approved the method? (The owner's current answer is no.)
