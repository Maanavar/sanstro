### Three traps in these pages — do not extract mechanically

1. **The Gana table on p. 72 is wrong without the errata.** As printed it gives
   Manushya Ganam five asterisms, so Deva (9) + Manushya (5) + Rakshasa (9) = 23,
   not 27. The **errata note on the contents page** supplies the missing four:
   *"Page 72: Utharashada, Utharapalguni, Purvashada, Purvabadhrapadha are also
   asterisms of Manushya Ganam."* With it the split is 9/9/9. **Anyone encoding
   p. 72 without reading the front matter ships a broken Gana table** — and
   `GANA_BY_NAKSHATRA` in [`porutham.py:36`](../app/calculations/porutham.py#L36)
   should be diffed against the corrected list, not the printed one.
2. **The Yoni page contradicts itself.** p. 73's hostile list names
   **Serpent and Mongoose**, but that page's own table never assigns Mongoose to
   any asterism — it folds Utharashada into Cow. Our engine assigns Utharashada
   = Mongoose and carries Serpent–Mongoose, so **we are not missing a pair**; the
   page is short one yoni. Its hostile list also gives **eight** pairs where we
   ship seven, differing in three places (it has Deer–Elephant and Serpent–Rat;
   we have Elephant–Lion). This is the "27 is odd, so a dropped edge hides"
   pattern that already caught us once on Vedha. **Do not encode p. 73 from the
   transcription** — it needs the physical page and a ruling.
3. **A bare "p. 243" is now ambiguous.** [`sade_sati.py:11`](../app/calculations/sade_sati.py#L11)
   cites "(p.243)" with no book named. Kalaprakasika's **Appendix also begins at
   printed p. 243**. Two books in play, both with that page, one citation naming
   neither — precisely the failure the register exists to prevent. That citation
   needs its book before it needs its edition.

## How to shoot a page so it actually closes a row

1. **Include the title page and the imprint line in the same batch.** A page
   number without an edition closes nothing.
2. **Shoot the whole page**, not the cropped table. Surrounding lines are
   repeatedly what settled a question here: p. 70's closing line gave the Vedha
   triad; Ch. XXIV's closing line proved a Purnima reversal was deliberate.
3. **Shoot the front matter, including errata.** The Gana trap above is a
   errata-only correction. A table extracted without it is wrong and looks right.
4. **Shoot the facing page** where a rule runs on — this book has been caught
   reversing itself one page later (Leo best for sowing, avoided for tillage,
   p. 101) and three pages later on a sign.
5. **Record the printed page number visible in the shot**, and state whether you
   are reading printed or PDF numbering. For Kalaprakasika the offset is **+32**.
6. **When a row closes, put the four fields in the code comment at the site**,
   not only in the register.
