## For the content reviewer: life-stage wording (S1)

**What happened.** Each page used to say "ages 13–15", "in your 40s" and so on.
Those numbers were removed and a test now blocks any digit or decade word from
coming back. But the text still uses **life-stage words**: *school, college,
university, adolescence, young adulthood, mid-life, marriage, children,
grandchildren, retirement.* These are timing claims too. No automated check
can tell whether they are true.

**Why it matters.** For a given star, a dasa can fall at very different ages
depending on the pada (how much of the first dasa was left at birth). For
example, Bharani's Moon dasa comes in adolescence for the later padas and in
adult life for the earlier ones. A sentence that says "this is the school
years" may be true for one pada and false for another.

**What to check, page by page.** The review packet gives each star the
engine's **age table** (when each dasa starts and ends, for each pada), followed
by the rewritten text. For each life-stage word:

1. Look at the age table for that dasa.
2. Is the word true for **every** pada? If yes, OK.
3. If it is true only for some padas, does the sentence say so? (e.g. "for the
   later padas …", "where the balance reaches the school years …"). If yes,
   OK.
4. If neither, mark it, and suggest either a conditional wording or a
   life-stage-free wording.

Please also look for wording that is too certain ("will", "always") where a
tendency is meant. That is release condition 2.

**Reply:** per page, either "condition 3 OK" or a list of the sentences that
need changing.
