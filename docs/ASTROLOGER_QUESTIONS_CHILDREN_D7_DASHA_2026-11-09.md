## Why we are asking

Today the app judges both doshams from the ராசிக் கட்டம் (birth chart) alone:

- **Putra Sarpa:** declared only when Rahu or Ketu is in the 5th house. It is graded up when a node
  also joins the 5th lord, or joins Jupiter when Jupiter is linked to the 5th. A strong 5th lord or
  Jupiter in a kendra lowers the grade by one, and never cancels it.
- **Santhana Thadai:** formed by Saturn. A guarded 5th house gives a lighter parihara.

The owner ruled on 2026-10-30 that the order is: formation, then protection, then **சப்தாம்சம் (D7)
and the running dasa, once you define them**.

On 2026-11-09 we built both checks from standard practice, and they now show on each card:

- **D7:** does the dosham's own planet (Rahu/Ketu, or Saturn) sit in the D7 5th or with its lord
  (the pattern *repeats*)? Is the D7 5th lord in the 6th, 8th or 12th? Does Jupiter support the
  D7 5th (in it, aspecting it, or in a kendra or trikona, free of the nodes)? No verdict is given
  when the birth time is uncertain by more than 2 minutes, or the D7 lagna is within 2 minutes of
  changing (about one chart in four). Is 2 minutes the right line?
- **Dasa:** the periods of the afflicting planet are "felt most"; Jupiter and the 5th lord are
  "supportive" unless the dosham itself joins them; the card lists the dated windows for the
  next 20 years.

**They do not change the grade or the parihara** until you confirm them. The questions below are
how you confirm, correct or replace them.

The owner also ruled that **a weak D7 is not a leg** (it cannot declare or grade up the dosham on its
own), because no text we had defined one. Please say if you would change that.

## 2. The dasa check

The card already says when a dosham is "most noticeable", for example "in the dasa or bhukti of Ketu".
We need to know whether the dasa also changes the reading itself.

- Which periods bring a children's dosham into effect? Rahu or Ketu (Putra Sarpa), Saturn (Santhana
  Thadai), the 5th lord, Jupiter, a planet sitting in the 5th? Please list the ones you use.
- Is the bhukti enough, or does it have to be the mahadasa?
- Does the running dasa ever **change the grade**, or does it only say **when** the dosham shows?
- If the person is past the age of having children, or the relevant dasa has already passed,
  should the card say so?
