- **Putra Sarpa** is declared only when Rahu or Ketu is **in the 5th house**. Alone it is *mild*.

  lord and a protecting Jupiter. Both definitions are stand-ins (B6, B7).
- The order is: formation, then protection, then the D7 and the running dasa. The D7 and dasa
  checks are built and shown on the card, but **they change nothing** until you confirm them. So

two-chart (marriage) reading: a comparable dosham in the other chart, or Mars debilitated in
கடகம். A single chart never reads "cancelled".
